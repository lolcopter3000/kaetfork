    Upcoming events... 

    • [CANCELLED!!] Bk Pepper

      — BK Pepper

      May 29 / 7.30pm (Doors) - 8pm (Start) / 1 Little Green Street / 10euro pay what you can

      To mark the release of Pagan, BK Pepper presents an intimate live performance in Dublin.

      Known for his cinematic sound and work across film and orchestral composition, BK Pepper brings this new body of work into a stripped back, close setting. A chance to hear music from Pagan performed live, alongside selected pieces that trace the journey to this record. Blending orchestral textures, voice and atmosphere, the album explores themes of belief, connection and shared experience. A quiet, immersive night of new music, up close.

    • Utsav Lal

      May 31 / 7.30pm (Doors) - 8pm (Start) / 1 Little Green Street / 10 euro

      A concert of highly personal solo piano improvisations, original compositions and explorations featuring two sets from New York based raga pianist Utsav Lal and Dublin based Daniel Luke.
